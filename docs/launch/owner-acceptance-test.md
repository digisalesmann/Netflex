# Owner acceptance test

Work through this on your own phone and laptop. It takes about 30 minutes. Tick each line, and note anything that looks wrong or confusing.

Paid Wi-Fi is still off, so part 2 checks the buying screens without charging anyone. The real purchase happens on launch day (launch checklist, section D).

## 1. The public website (phone)

- [ ] `netflexhq.com` opens quickly and looks right: lodge name, photos, plans
- [ ] **Plans** shows Daily ₦400, Weekly ₦2,500 and Monthly ₦8,000
- [ ] **How it works**, **FAQ**, **Coverage** and **Contact** read correctly for ND Lodge
- [ ] Terms, Privacy and Refunds (bottom of the page) open and say what you agreed
- [ ] **Contact**: send a test message, then find it in Office → Support

## 2. A resident's account (phone)

First, in Office → Residents, add yourself as a resident in a test room, using an email you can open.

- [ ] Sign in at `netflexhq.com` with that email: the code email arrives within a minute and looks right
- [ ] The account shows your room and the plans
- [ ] Choosing a plan explains that online buying isn't open yet (paid access is off)
- [ ] **Settings**: email preferences can be turned on and off
- [ ] Sign out asks you to confirm

## 3. The office (laptop)

- [ ] Sign in at `netflexhq.com/admin` with your password and authenticator code
- [ ] **Overview** shows the right numbers of rooms and residents
- [ ] **Rooms**: the 3 floors and 31 rooms are right
- [ ] **Vouchers → Make vouchers**: make one Daily code (note "acceptance test"); it shows as unused
- [ ] **Staff**: you're listed as owner; add the second owner
- [ ] **Activity**: the voucher and the staff change are listed with your name
- [ ] **Settings**: the lodge phone and email are right; your devices are listed
- [ ] Every dropdown opens and picks correctly; every sign-out asks first
- [ ] Now on a phone: the office menu and pages fit the screen

## 4. Clean up

- [ ] Cancel the test voucher (Vouchers → the code → **Cancel code**)
- [ ] Move your test resident out (Residents → you → **Record move-out**)

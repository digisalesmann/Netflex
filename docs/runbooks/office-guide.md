# Office guide: running ND Lodge Wi-Fi

For the lodge's office staff. The office is at **netflexhq.com/admin**. Sign in with your email, your password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

## Every morning (2 minutes)

1. Open **Overview**. If there's anything under **Needs attention**, click it and deal with it (see below).
2. Open **Support** and answer anything that's **New**.

## Common jobs

### A new resident moves in

**Residents → Add resident.** Fill in their name, email and phone, and pick their room and move-in date. They can then sign in at netflexhq.com with their email and buy a plan.

### A resident moves room or leaves

Open the resident → **Move room**, or **Record move-out** with their last day. Plans they already paid for run until they end.

### Giving someone free Wi-Fi (a guest, an apology, a staff member)

**Vouchers → Make vouchers.** Pick the plan and how many, write a note (it's saved in the activity log, e.g. "paid cash, room 12"), and give the person the code. Codes are only shown once, so copy them or write them down before closing.

### "I paid but didn't get a code"

**Payments**, then search the order number (starts with NFX-) or the reference on their Paystack receipt.

- **Paid:** open it to see the order. The resident can see the code in their account under **Orders**.
- **Pending:** Paystack hasn't confirmed it yet. The system checks again by itself within a few minutes.
- **Not there:** ask for the Paystack receipt and pass it to the owner.

### "My code doesn't work"

Check the code was typed exactly (no spaces), and that they're on the lodge's guest Wi-Fi. Then open **Vouchers** and search the code's last 4 characters: it shows whether it's unused, in use, ended, suspended or cancelled. If a code was lost or seen by someone else, use **Replace code**.

### Refunds

**Payments** → the payment → **Refund**: enter the amount (part refunds are fine) and the reason, then **Request refund**. A **different** manager or owner must then **Approve** it under **Payments → Refunds**, so no one person can send money out alone. Paystack returns the money to the card in a few working days. Follow the refund policy on the website.

### "The Wi-Fi is down"

1. Check one floor's access point has its light on, and the switch and Starlink router have power.
2. **Network** shows whether the equipment is answering.
3. Restart in this order, waiting 2 minutes between each: Starlink router → switch → the access point.
4. If it's still down, call the engineer and the owner.

## Emails the owner gets

The system emails the owners (at most once a day per problem) when:

| Email                                     | What to do                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Wi-Fi equipment isn't answering           | Follow "The Wi-Fi is down" above                                                                             |
| Some plans couldn't be switched on or off | Open **Network** → **Try again** on the failed jobs; call the engineer if they keep failing                  |
| Payments need checking                    | Open **Payments → Mismatches**; compare each one with Paystack, then **Mark as resolved** with what happened |
| A refund didn't go through                | Open **Payments → Refunds**; check in Paystack, try again or contact Paystack support                        |

## Keeping the office safe

- Never share your password or your authenticator app. Everyone has their own account.
- New phone? Ask an owner to reset your sign-in, then scan the new code.
- Leaving a shared computer: sign out (top right) and confirm.
- A staff member leaves: an owner disables them in **Staff** the same day.
- Everything you do is recorded under **Activity**.
